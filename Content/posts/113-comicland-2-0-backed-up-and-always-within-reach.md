<p>Rounding out ComicLand 2.0: your collection is safer, and getting to it takes fewer taps.</p>
<h2>Back up anywhere</h2>
<p>Back up and restore the collection to and from Documents, including iCloud Drive and Dropbox &mdash; your data isn&#8217;t stuck on one device.</p>
<h2>Quicker access from the Home Screen</h2>
<p>A new Notification Center widget gives quick access to the most recently added series. Long-press the Home Screen icon to add a comic, search Comic Vine, or jump straight to Recent Issues or the Wishlist &mdash; no need to open the app and navigate first.</p>
<h2>Share what you&#8217;re reading</h2>
<p>Share an issue&#8217;s details via Facebook, Twitter, email, or Messages, straight from the comic itself.</p>
