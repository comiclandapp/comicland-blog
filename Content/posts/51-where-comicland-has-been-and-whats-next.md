<p>It&#8217;s been a big stretch of growth for ComicLand, so we wanted to take a step back — both to look at how far the app has come recently, and to give a peek at what&#8217;s happening behind the scenes right now.</p>
<h2>A year of growth</h2>
<p>Reading Maps got a real engine behind them, automatically detecting reprint and continuity references in a comic&#8217;s description and turning them into suggested reading orders. Comics gained a fuller physical picture too — separate front, back, spine, and dust-jacket cover images, a &#8220;Has Dust Jacket&#8221; toggle, and new relationship types like &#8220;Reprinted/Collected in&#8221; and &#8220;Continues From/To&#8221; for tracking how issues and volumes connect. Collection Statistics arrived, giving a real dashboard of spending by year, month, and publisher, with drill-down into individual purchases and, later, a breakdown by release year.</p>
<p>Then came the provider expansion. <strong>BDGest</strong> and <strong>Whakoom</strong> joined as full providers, followed by <strong>Grand Comics Database</strong> and <strong>Comic Vine</strong> being promoted from simple search sources into full providers with their own weekly new-release feeds and detail screens — GCD even gained direct sync with a user&#8217;s real my.comics.org wishlist and collection. Most recently, <strong>BubbleBD</strong> joined as a fifth provider, with its own rankings, new releases, wishlist, and collection pages. Across all of them, a unified &#8220;Add to ComicLand&#8221; action and &#8220;already in your library&#8221; indicators turned browsing external sources and building your collection into one connected workflow instead of separate steps.</p>
<p>More recently still, ComicLand added <strong>scan-to-search</strong> — a barcode scan now checks whether a comic&#8217;s already in your collection before you decide to add it — along with a brand-new first-launch onboarding tour with live interactive walkthroughs, and a barcode field alongside ISBN for more precise cataloging.</p>
<h2>What we&#8217;re building next</h2>
<p>Right now, work is underway on a deeper rebuild of how comic, series, and publisher detail screens load and render — faster, more reliable, and laying the groundwork for what&#8217;s coming. That same performance work is being extended across the rest of the library (Comics, Series, Publishers, Creators, Characters).</p>
<p>We&#8217;re also building a proper backend for ComicLand — the foundation for account-based sync between your iPhone and a browser. It&#8217;s early days: right now it can take a backup of your existing collection and let you browse it in a web &#8220;Collection&#8221; view organized the same way the app is, with filtering, sorting, and layout options. There&#8217;s a lot still ahead — richer detail pages, better image handling, and eventually true two-way sync between the app and the web — but it&#8217;s the first real step toward using ComicLand outside of just your phone.</p>
<p>More to come as both pieces mature. Thanks for following along!</p>
