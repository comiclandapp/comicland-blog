<p>ComicLand 5.0 fills in some long-missing detail in the comic editor &mdash; for hardcovers with real spines and dust jackets, and for faster, smarter credit-taking.</p>
<h2>Spine and flap cover images</h2>
<p>Add and edit a spine image for a comic, shown as a thin strip between the back and front covers in the comic editor and on the details page in landscape. Front and back dust-jacket flap images work the same way, shown alongside the front, back, and spine covers. For books where all of that is really one continuous piece of art, turn on <strong>wraparound cover</strong> so the pieces sit flush against each other instead of with gaps.</p>
<h2>Has Dust Jacket</h2>
<p>A new toggle in the comic editor, and a matching indicator on the details page, tracks whether a hardcover still has its dust jacket.</p>
<h2>Faster credits</h2>
<p>Bulk-add everyone already credited across a series&#8217; other issues when editing a comic&#8217;s Creators or Characters, instead of adding each one by hand. And the Creators/Characters picker itself got a redesign &mdash; recent and existing entries now show as compact cards with cover art, grouped and sorted to match the main Creators/Characters tab.</p>
