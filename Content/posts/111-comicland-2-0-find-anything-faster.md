<p>The other half of ComicLand 2.0 was about organizing and finding what&#8217;s actually in your collection &mdash; more ways to filter, more things to edit, and more ways to browse.</p>
<h2>More statuses to filter by</h2>
<p>The collection can now be filtered by status &mdash; <strong>All, Owned, Ordered, Wishlist, For Sale, For Trade, Loaned, Not in Collection,</strong> or <strong>Recent</strong>. Set how many comics show per series in the Comics list too (the default is four).</p>
<h2>A new Authors tab</h2>
<p>Authors get their own tab &mdash; every author, with editing and the ability to browse everything credited to them in one place.</p>
<h2>More things you can edit</h2>
<p>Aliases, cities, countries, crossovers, formats, roles, and story arcs are all now editable, and issues, series, publishers, and authors can all take custom tags.</p>
<h2>Peek before you open</h2>
<p>Press firmly on a series, publisher, author, or Comic Vine volume to peek at a preview, then press again to open it &mdash; 3D Touch made browsing quicker without a full navigation push. And every issue in the collection is now indexed by Spotlight, so it turns up in iOS search too.</p>
