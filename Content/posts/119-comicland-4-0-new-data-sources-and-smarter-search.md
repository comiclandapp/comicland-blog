<p>Version 4.0 didn&#8217;t just rebuild ComicLand from the ground up — it opened the app to a second real data source, and made search and refresh work across both.</p>
<h2>League of Comic Geeks joins Comic Vine</h2>
<p>League of Comic Geeks is now a data source alongside Comic Vine — browse and import from it, though today it&#8217;s a one-way download rather than a live sync.</p>
<h2>Search and refresh, everywhere</h2>
<p>Search for comics, series, publishers, and creators on both Comic Vine and League of Comic Geeks, and refresh any of them — comics, series, publishers, creators — from whichever provider they came from.</p>
<p>Comic Vine imports also got richer: they now bring in teams, concepts, locations, and objects, not just issues and creators.</p>
