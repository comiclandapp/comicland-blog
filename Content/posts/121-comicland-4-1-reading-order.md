<p>ComicLand 4.1&#8217;s headline feature is an experimental new way to see how a series actually fits together.</p>
<h2>An interactive graph of your whole series</h2>
<p><strong>Reading Order</strong> visualizes comics and series as nodes, laid out vertically in reading order. Open it from a comic&#8217;s detail screen and it highlights the current issue with an orange border, so you can instantly see where you are in the series.</p>
<p>You can pan, pinch-to-zoom, and tap any node to jump straight to that comic. A mini-map in the corner lets you jump around the full graph, and a series filter in the menu narrows the view to a single series when you don&#8217;t need the whole picture at once.</p>
