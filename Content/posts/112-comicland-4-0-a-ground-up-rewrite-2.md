<p>ComicLand 4.0 is the biggest release in the app&#8217;s history — not because of any one feature, but because of what&#8217;s underneath all of them.</p>
<h2>Every line, rewritten in Swift</h2>
<p>The entire app is now written in Swift, top to bottom. That alone is worth pausing on: this wasn&#8217;t a partial migration or a bridge between old and new code, it&#8217;s a full rewrite of the app you&#8217;ve been using.</p>
<h2>Liquid Glass and full Dark Mode</h2>
<p>ComicLand adopts Apple&#8217;s new <strong>Liquid Glass</strong> design language, and now supports full <strong>Dark Mode</strong> throughout — not just a handful of screens, everywhere.</p>
<h2>Speaking your language</h2>
<p>The app now supports <strong>14 languages</strong>: English, Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Dutch, Portuguese (Brazil), Portuguese (Portugal), Russian, Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), and Chinese (Hong Kong).</p>
<p>More on what else shipped in 4.0 in the posts that follow.</p>
