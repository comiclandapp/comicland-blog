<p>Before Providers, before Geeks or BDGest or Whakoom, before dark mode or widgets &mdash; there was just ComicLand 1.0.</p>
<p>The first release did one thing: catalog and track a comic collection. No syncing, no imports from other sites, just a place to record what you own. Every feature since has built on that same foundation &mdash; a personal, offline-first record of your collection that&#8217;s actually yours.</p>
<p>Looking back at the earliest changelog is a reminder of how far the app has come since.</p>
