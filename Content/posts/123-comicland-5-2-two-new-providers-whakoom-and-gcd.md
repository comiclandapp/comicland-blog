<p>ComicLand 5.2 connects to two more comics databases, plus a couple of small fixes that make signing in everywhere feel more consistent.</p>
<h2>New Provider: Whakoom</h2>
<p>Connect your <strong>Whakoom</strong> account and browse your comic collection right in ComicLand, with cover art, incremental loading, pull-to-refresh, and direct links back to each comic on Whakoom.</p>
<p>The Whakoom tab also gained an <strong>Explore</strong> section &mdash; browse Whakoom&#8217;s Popular, Top Rated, and Graphic Novels catalogs with the same list/grid layout and quick &#8220;Add to ComicLand&#8221; you already use elsewhere.</p>
<h2>New Provider: GCD</h2>
<p>ComicLand now connects to <strong>comics.org</strong> (the Grand Comics Database) too. Browse a weekly On Sale list, sign in to sync your real Wishlist and Collection, and quick-add comics with a long-press &mdash; the same in-app comic details, cover-tap lightbox, and list/grid layouts every other provider already has.</p>
<h2>Smoother sign-in and loading</h2>
<p>Geeks, BDGest, and Whakoom now show the same sign-in prompt when you&#8217;re not logged in, and pop the sign-in sheet right away instead of leaving the screen blank. Loading screens that could get stuck or briefly flash an empty message before results arrived are fixed too.</p>
<p>Whakoom&#8217;s comic detail page also parses authors, language, publish date, and rating more accurately, and opening a series &mdash; or My Series itself &mdash; no longer re-walks your entire Whakoom collection every time. It&#8217;s served from cache instantly, with My Series now showing proper square series cards instead of stretched comic covers.</p>
