<p>The Today widget was redesigned in 5.5. Here is what it shows at each size.</p>
<h2>Small</h2>
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/widget-small-2.jpg" alt="ComicLand small widget showing the most recently added series cover and title" /></figure>
<p>The small widget now leads with real cover art instead of a generic icon &mdash; your most recently added series, front and center, with a single tap that opens straight to it in ComicLand.</p>
<h2>Medium</h2>
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/widget-medium.jpg" alt="ComicLand medium widget showing three recent series covers in a row" /></figure>
<p>Medium widens the view to your three most recent series, each with its own cover and title, so a quick glance at your Home Screen tells you what you&#8217;ve been adding lately.</p>
<h2>Large</h2>
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/widget-large.jpg" alt="ComicLand large widget showing three recent series with publisher, year, genre, and country flag" /></figure>
<p>Large shows cover, title, publisher and year, genre tags, and a small flag for the series&#8217; country of origin. Tap any row and it opens that exact series in ComicLand.</p>
<p>Across every size, cover loading is more reliable and taps now reliably land on the right series &mdash; details in the <a href="/2026/09/29/whats-new-in-comicland-5-5/">What&#8217;s New in ComicLand 5.5</a> post.</p>
