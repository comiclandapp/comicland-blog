<p>ComicLand 5.1 is a smaller release with two real wins &mdash; a new data source, and long-overdue translation work.</p>
<h2>New provider: BDGest</h2>
<p>Added support for <strong>BDGest</strong> (bdgest.com), a French-language comics database &mdash; browse your wishlist and collection, search for comics and series, and import them directly into ComicLand. Comics and series linked to BDGest show a tappable icon linking to their page on bedetheque.com.</p>
<h2>Role &amp; power localization</h2>
<p>Creator roles (Writer, Artist, Colorist, and more) and character powers are now translated throughout the app &mdash; including League of Comic Geeks creator cards, which previously showed raw English text no matter your language setting. A new &#8220;Clean Up Duplicates&#8221; option in Database settings also merges duplicate or near-duplicate power and role names in one pass.</p>
