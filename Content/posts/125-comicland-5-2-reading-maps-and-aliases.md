<p>Two more additions in ComicLand 5.2 help you make sense of a large library: a way to map how comics connect, and a way to make sure you find the right creator or series no matter what name you know it by.</p>
<h2>Reading maps</h2>
<p>Build and save reading maps from explicit Continues From/To and Reprinted/Collected In links across your entire library. Series See Also links expand the connected map, and once a map is built, rebuilt maps reopen instantly without another scan.</p>
<h2>Add Aliases</h2>
<p>Attach alternate names to creators, publishers, and series, so they&#8217;re easier to find and match under names you already know them by &mdash; useful for creators with pen names, publishers that have rebranded, or series with regional title differences.</p>
