<p>We&#8217;re excited to share that ComicLand has been accepted by Apple and is now available to download on the App Store.</p>
<p>ComicLand is your all-in-one companion for tracking and organizing your comic book collection &mdash; whether you&#8217;re cataloging longboxes of back issues or following the latest weekly releases.</p>
<p><strong>ComicLand runs everywhere you do:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>iPhone</strong> &mdash; take your collection with you</li>
<li><strong>iPad</strong> &mdash; a bigger canvas for browsing and organizing</li>
<li><strong>Mac</strong> &mdash; runs natively on Apple Silicon Macs, no separate download needed</li>
</ul>
<p>Download it today:</p>
