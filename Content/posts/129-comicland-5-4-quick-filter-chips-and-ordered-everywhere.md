<p>ComicLand 5.4 is a small, focused release &mdash; two changes that make everyday filtering and adding comics a little quicker.</p>
<h2>Quick-Filter Chips</h2>
<p>Comics and Series now show Owned, Ordered, and Wishlist chips below the stats header. Tap one to filter instantly, without opening the &bull;&bull;&bull; menu.</p>
<h2>Ordered Quick-Add</h2>
<p>Every provider&#8217;s &#8220;Add to ComicLand&#8221; menu &mdash; Comic Vine, League of Comic Geeks, BDGest, Whakoom, BubbleBD, and GCD &mdash; now offers Ordered alongside Owned and Wishlist, in the same order everywhere.</p>
