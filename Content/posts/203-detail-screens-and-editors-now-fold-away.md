<p>Comic, Series, Publisher, and Person screens have been regrouped into named sections — General, Edition, Collection, and so on — and every one of them folds away with a tap on its chevron.</p>
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/collapsible-sections-scaled.png" alt="A comic detail screen showing General, Edition, and Collection sections, each with a collapse chevron"/><figcaption>Sections on a comic&#8217;s detail screen, each independently collapsible.</figcaption></figure>
<p>Your choices stick. Collapse the sections you never look at and they stay collapsed the next time you open that kind of screen, so a long record becomes as short as you want it.</p>
<h2>Detail screens and editors match</h2>
<p>The detail screens and their editors now use matching section groupings. Previously a comic&#8217;s <em>detail</em> view organised fields one way and its <em>editor</em> another, so you had to re-learn the layout every time you moved between reading and editing. Series&#8217; Relationships and Person&#8217;s Biography have been folded into Details for the same reason — fewer, better-named places to look.</p>
<p>Smaller things that came along with it: a Read row on the Series detail screen&#8217;s History section, Favorite rows on Series and Publisher, and a status icon on a comic&#8217;s Information row that matches how Country already showed its flag.</p>
