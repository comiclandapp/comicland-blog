<p class="wp-block-paragraph">ComicLand 5.4 is officially submitted and now sitting with Apple for review. Here&#8217;s what&#8217;s coming once it&#8217;s approved.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Quick-filter chips</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Comics and Series now show <strong>Owned</strong>, <strong>Ordered</strong>, and <strong>Wishlist</strong> chips right below the stats header — tap one to filter instantly, without opening the ••• menu&#8217;s Filter by Status option.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Ordered joins quick-add</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Every provider&#8217;s &#8220;Add to ComicLand&#8221; menu — Comic Vine, League of Comic Geeks, BDGest, Whakoom, BubbleBD, and GCD — now offers <strong>Ordered</strong> alongside Owned and Wishlist, consistently ordered the same way everywhere.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We&#8217;ll post again once it&#8217;s live. Thanks for following along!</p>
