<p>Rounding out version 4.0: a handful of changes aimed squarely at how you actually use the app day to day.</p>
<h2>Comic Priority</h2>
<p>Set and filter comics by priority — None, Low, Medium, High, or Buy Next — so your most-wanted books don&#8217;t get lost in a big collection.</p>
<h2>Comic Stories</h2>
<p>A new Stories tab on the comic details page lists every story within that issue.</p>
<h2>Sorting, layout, and tab swiping</h2>
<p>Many new sorting and layout options were added across the app, and you can now swipe left or right to switch between tabs instead of tapping each one.</p>
