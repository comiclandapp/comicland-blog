<p>Version 3.1 is a milestone release in ComicLand&#8217;s history — the first real step toward the modern, all-Swift app it is today.</p>
<h2>Keeping up with iOS 13</h2>
<p>A handful of small adjustments kept ComicLand running well on iOS 13.</p>
<h2>Dark Mode</h2>
<p>ComicLand gained support for Dark Mode throughout the app.</p>
<h2>The start of the Swift conversion</h2>
<p>The Today widget, backup format, localization, What&#8217;s New, and Settings were all rebuilt in Swift — the first pieces of a longer effort toward a faster, more reliable app that would eventually reach every corner of ComicLand.</p>
