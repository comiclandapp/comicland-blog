<p>ComicLand 2.0 was a big release, and a lot of it was about covers &mdash; loading them faster, viewing them better, and no longer needing a separate copy of every image just to use it.</p>
<h2>Point at a photo instead of copying it</h2>
<p>New in 2.0: a comic&#8217;s cover can point at a Photos asset instead of importing a copy of the image into the app. One less duplicate sitting on your device, one less thing to manage.</p>
<h2>A real iPad layout</h2>
<p>iPad got its own layout pass &mdash; wider margins, with front and back cover views shown together in the issue header instead of squeezed into a layout built for a phone screen.</p>
<h2>Browse and view</h2>
<p>Tap an issue&#8217;s cover, then swipe to browse every cover in that series without leaving the viewer. Long-press a full-size cover image to copy it to the pasteboard. And in edit mode, tap an image to crop, rotate, and make other adjustments right there.</p>
<h2>Faster, too</h2>
<p>Cover images now load noticeably faster while scrolling &mdash; the first of many performance passes the app would get over the years.</p>
