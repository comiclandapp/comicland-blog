<p>ComicLand 5.3 adds a third French-language comics database to the list of connected providers.</p>
<h2>New Provider: BubbleBD</h2>
<p>Connect to <strong>bubblebd.com</strong> &mdash; browse Top Sales and New Releases, sign in to download your real Wishlist and Collection, and quick-add comics with a long-press, with the same in-app comic details, cover-tap lightbox, and list/grid layouts every other provider already has.</p>
<p>Comics linked to BubbleBD show a tappable icon back to their page on bubblebd.com, so you can jump out to the source whenever you need to.</p>
