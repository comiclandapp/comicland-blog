<p>The rest of ComicLand 5.0 is smaller, everyday stuff &mdash; the kind that adds up.</p>
<h2>Tap to browse</h2>
<p>Aliases, tags, roles, and powers on series, publisher, comic, creator, and character pages are now tappable, jumping straight to everything else that shares that value.</p>
<h2>Swipe to delete recent searches</h2>
<p>Swipe left on a recent search &mdash; in the main search, Comic Vine search, Geeks search, or GCD search &mdash; to remove just that one, the same way Safari lets you delete a single history item.</p>
<h2>Smarter, faster series covers</h2>
<p>When a series has no cover of its own, the fallback now prefers a single issue&#8217;s cover, then a trade paperback&#8217;s, and finally a hardcover&#8217;s &mdash; and loads faster, since the result is no longer recomputed every time you scroll.</p>
<h2>Two fixes</h2>
<p>A long comic title no longer pushes the plot summary off the card &mdash; the card now grows to fit both. And League of Comic Geeks imports no longer show an untranslated &#8220;Western Frontier&#8221; genre label.</p>
