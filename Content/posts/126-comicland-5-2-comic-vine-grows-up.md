<p>Comic Vine&#8217;s corner of ComicLand got a lot bigger in 5.2, with new ways to browse and a display upgrade that now applies across every provider.</p>
<h2>New Comics, Popular Comics &amp; Popular Series</h2>
<p>Comic Vine now has three browsable segments: a weekly <strong>New Comics</strong> feed, plus Comic Vine&#8217;s own <strong>Popular Comics</strong> and <strong>Popular Series</strong> rankings, each showing up to 100 items. Browse any of them in list or grid layout, with quick-add and tap-through to full comic and series details.</p>
<h2>Cover-tap lightbox everywhere</h2>
<p>Tapping a cover on Comic Vine, BDGest, and Whakoom cards now opens the same swipeable full-screen lightbox other covers already use.</p>
<h2>Comic variant import &amp; display</h2>
<p>Import comic variants from provider lists and see the parent issue and its cover variants together in Home, series, and timeline views.</p>
