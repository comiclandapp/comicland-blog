<p>A step back into ComicLand&#8217;s history — version 2.3 was a small, practical release focused on smoothing out day-to-day use.</p>
<h2>Keeping up with iOS 11</h2>
<p>A handful of small adjustments kept ComicLand running well on iOS 11.</p>
<h2>Backups go through the system file browser</h2>
<p>Backups switched to using the system&#8217;s own file browser to choose where a backup is saved or restored from, instead of a custom picker.</p>
<h2>Straight to Issues from the Wishlist</h2>
<p>Tapping a series from the Wishlist started opening straight to its Issues tab, instead of the series overview — one less tap to get to what you actually wanted to see.</p>
