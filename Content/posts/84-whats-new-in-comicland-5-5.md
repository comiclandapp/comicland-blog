<p>ComicLand 5.5 brings a redesigned iPad and Mac experience, a richer widget, and a new way to see a comic&#8217;s status at a glance across every provider you use.</p>
<h2>iPad &amp; Mac Sidebar</h2>
<p>iPad and Mac now show a sidebar instead of tabs &mdash; Home, Search, every provider, and Settings are one tap away, leaving more room next to it for your collection.</p>
<h2>Richer Recent Series Widget</h2>
<p>The Today widget now has richer recent-series cards, better cover loading, and taps that open the right series in ComicLand.</p>
<h2>Provider Status Badges</h2>
<p>Comic cards now show small status badges directly on their provider icons. See at a glance if a comic is owned or wishlisted on League of Comic Geeks or Whakoom, and whether it&#8217;s already in your ComicLand collection when browsing any provider&#8217;s own list &mdash; BDGest, BubbleBD, GCD, Comic Vine, Geeks, or Whakoom.</p>
