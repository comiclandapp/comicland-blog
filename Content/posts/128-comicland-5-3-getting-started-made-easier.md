<p>Alongside BubbleBD, 5.3 ships a handful of changes aimed at first-time installs and everyday navigation.</p>
<h2>Guided Tour</h2>
<p>A short guided tour now walks first-time installs through browsing modes, adding a comic, the &bull;&bull;&bull; menu, the Providers tab, and Search. You can replay it anytime from Settings&#8217; &bull;&bull;&bull; menu.</p>
<h2>Scan to Search</h2>
<p>Scan a comic&#8217;s barcode from the Search tab to jump straight to it in your collection.</p>
<h2>Blog link</h2>
<p>About now links to the ComicLand blog at comicland.net &mdash; which, fittingly, is exactly where you&#8217;re reading this.</p>
<h2>Home search tap fix</h2>
<p>Fixed a bug where tapping the last card in a short search results list could get stuck bouncing the navigation and tab bars instead of opening it.</p>
