<p>ComicLand 5.0 goes beyond just tracking what you own &mdash; it starts helping you understand your collection, and bring older ones back into the fold.</p>
<h2>Collection Statistics</h2>
<p>A new dashboard, reachable from Home&#8217;s &bull;&bull;&bull; menu, gives you a real overview of your collection: spending broken down by year, month, and publisher, and your most expensive purchases. Tap any card to see the comics behind the numbers.</p>
<h2>Reprints &amp; Collected Editions</h2>
<p>Link a comic to the trade paperback or omnibus that collects it, and back again, right from the comic editor. Candidates are matched by series title, and the link appears as tappable chips on both comics &mdash; so jumping between a single issue and the collection that reprints it is one tap either way.</p>
<h2>Import Legacy Backups</h2>
<p>Restoring a backup from ComicLand v2.1 (2014-2015) now migrates it straight into the current database format, so long-time users can finally bring their original collection forward.</p>
