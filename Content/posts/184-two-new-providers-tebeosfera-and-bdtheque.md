<p>ComicLand now talks to two more comic databases, bringing the total to eight providers you can search, browse, and import from right inside the app.</p>
<h2>Tebeosfera</h2>
<p>Tebeosfera is a major Spanish-language comics reference site. Search covers both collections and individual issues, kept scoped the way the site itself organizes them, and long-press any result to add it straight to your ComicLand collection. Novedades &#8212; new releases &#8212; is split into Upcoming, Last Month, and By Month, with real pagination instead of one long scroll.</p>
<h2>BDTheque</h2>
<p>BDTheque is a long-running French-language database for bandes dessin&#233;es. Browse its latest additions, new releases, and must-read series, filter those must-reads by genre and category, and switch between list and grid views depending on how you like to scan a shelf. Sign in once in Settings &#8594; Credentials and ComicLand remembers you.</p>
<p>Both join the six providers already in ComicLand &#8212; Comic Vine, League of Comic Geeks, the Grand Comics Database, BDGest, BubbleBD, and Whakoom &#8212; all searchable and importable the same way.</p>
