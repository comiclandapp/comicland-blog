<p>Browsing a big synced collection or wishlist on a provider tab could mean a lot of scrolling to find one title.</p>
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/provider-search.jpg" alt="Geeks Collection tab showing the search bar open and filtering results for 007" /></figure>
<h2>A search icon next to the ••• menu</h2>
<p>Geeks, GCD, BDGest, BubbleBD, and Whakoom now each have a small search icon next to their ••• menu on collection and wishlist screens. Tap it and a search bar slides open &mdash; type a title and the list filters instantly, right there, without leaving the screen or losing your place.</p>
<p>It stays out of the way until you need it: collapsed by default, so it doesn&#8217;t eat into the list, and tapping the icon again closes it back up and clears the search.</p>
<h2>Filters what&#8217;s already loaded</h2>
<p>This searches your already-synced list by title, not a new server request &mdash; so it&#8217;s instant, and works the same whether you&#8217;re looking at Geeks&#8217; Subscriptions, Whakoom&#8217;s Collection, or any other provider&#8217;s own synced list.</p>
