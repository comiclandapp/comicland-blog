<p>The rest of 4.1 is smaller, practical fixes and one nice touch for collectors of older books.</p>
<h2>Legacy Currencies</h2>
<p>The currency picker now includes historical and obsolete currencies — Spanish Peseta, Dutch Guilder, German Deutsche Mark, French Franc, Italian Lira, and more — for pricing older comics accurately.</p>
<h2>Comic Vine, cleaned up</h2>
<p>Descriptions imported from Comic Vine now display as rich text with tappable links to the characters, creators, publishers, series, and issues they mention — jumping straight to that entity in the app, or opening Comic Vine in Safari for types not yet supported locally.</p>
<p>Also fixed: issue numbers that always imported as 0 from Comic Vine, and series stubs created from description links now automatically fetch their full data and issue list when tapped. Importing from a Comic Vine search or volume list shows the same &#8220;Refreshing from Comic Vine&#8221; spinner as a manual refresh.</p>
