<p>ComicLand now has an Apple Watch companion app &#8212; browse your series and flip through comic covers right from your wrist.</p>
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/comicland-watch-series-list.png" alt="ComicLand watch app showing a list of series with cover, publisher, and year" /></figure>
<p>Open the app on your watch and you get a list of your series, newest first, each with its cover, publisher, and release year. Tap into one and you get a card for every comic in it &#8212; cover, title, format, and whether it&#8217;s owned, ordered, or on your wishlist &#8212; and tapping a card shows that cover full-screen.</p>
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/comicland-watch-comic-card.png" alt="ComicLand watch app showing a comic card with cover, publisher, title, and format" /></figure>
<p>It syncs automatically with the ComicLand app on your phone, no setup required: open ComicLand on your iPhone at least once and your watch picks up the library on its own.</p>
