<p>Version 3.3 rebuilt a lot of what happens behind the scenes in ComicLand, setting the stage for everything that came after.</p>
<h2>Keeping up with iOS 17</h2>
<p>A handful of small adjustments kept ComicLand running well on iOS 17.</p>
<h2>Comic Vine search crash fix</h2>
<p>Fixed a crash that could happen while searching on Comic Vine.</p>
<h2>A new architecture</h2>
<p>Much of the app was rebuilt on a coordinator/view-model architecture, for more reliable navigation and faster future updates — the foundation a lot of later releases would build on.</p>
<h2>A new rating widget</h2>
<p>The old 0-5 star rating was replaced with a new 0-10 rating widget.</p>
