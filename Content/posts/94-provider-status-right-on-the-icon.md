<p>Comic cards in ComicLand already showed a row of provider icons &mdash; tap one to jump straight to that comic&#8217;s page on BDGest, BubbleBD, Whakoom, and the rest. In 5.5, those icons got more useful: each one can now carry a small status badge of its own.</p>
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/provider-status-badge.jpg" alt="A ComicLand comic card with a small status badge overlaid on the provider icon" /></figure>
<p>The badge in the corner of the provider icon shows whether that exact comic is already owned or on your wishlist, without opening the card or switching to that provider&#8217;s own tab.</p>
<h2>Both directions</h2>
<p>The badge works both ways. Browsing your own collection in ComicLand, a linked provider&#8217;s icon (League of Comic Geeks or Whakoom, so far) shows <em>that provider&#8217;s own</em> status for the comic. Flip it around, and browsing a provider&#8217;s own list &mdash; BDGest, BubbleBD, GCD, Comic Vine, Geeks, or Whakoom &mdash; the ComicLand icon shows whether that comic is already in your ComicLand collection, and what status it has there.</p>
<p>Same small badge, same status icons you already know from the rest of the app, wherever it shows up.</p>
<p>More on what shipped in this release in <a href="/2026/09/29/whats-new-in-comicland-5-5/">What&#8217;s New in ComicLand 5.5</a>.</p>
